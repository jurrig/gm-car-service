Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our car service.

How much does a ride from Sarasota to Tampa Airport (TPA) cost?

A sedan ride from Sarasota to Tampa International Airport starts at approximately $165. Use our instant quote tool for an exact fare based on your pickup location. SUV and luxury options are also available.

Do you provide service to Sarasota-Bradenton Airport (SRQ)?

Yes! We provide 24/7 airport transfers to and from SRQ. Enter your address in our quote tool to get an instant fare estimate.

What vehicles do you offer?

We offer three vehicle types: Sedan (up to 4 passengers, from $15 + $2.50/mile), SUV (up to 6 passengers, from $20 + $3.00/mile), and Luxury (up to 4 passengers, from $30 + $4.00/mile).

How do I book a ride?

Enter your pickup and drop-off addresses on our website to get an instant quote. Choose your vehicle, then provide your name and phone number to confirm. No upfront payment is required — pay at the time of service.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Sarasota County including Sarasota, Siesta Key, Longboat Key, Lakewood Ranch, Venice, and North Port. We also provide long-distance rides to Orlando, Fort Lauderdale, Palm Beach, and Miami, plus airport transfers to Tampa (TPA), St. Pete-Clearwater (PIE), Orlando (MCO), Fort Lauderdale (FLL), Palm Beach (PBI), and Miami (MIA).

Is your service available 24/7?

Yes, G&M Car Service operates around the clock, 7 days a week including holidays. Early morning airport runs and late-night pickups are no problem.
