Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 8, 2026

G&M Car Service, operated by LottoDeamon LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and transportation services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Booking Information: Name, phone number, email address, pickup/dropoff locations, travel dates, flight details, and number of passengers.
  • Payment Information: We do not store credit card numbers. Payments are processed through third-party providers (Zelle, cash, or other agreed methods).
  • Location Data: Pickup and dropoff addresses for route calculation and pricing. We do not track your real-time location.
  • Communication Data: SMS messages sent via our notification system (Twilio) for booking confirmations and ride updates.
  • Photos: Optional passenger photos for driver identification at airport pickups — stored securely and deleted after ride completion.
  • Usage Data: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your booking requests
  • To communicate ride confirmations, driver details, and pickup updates via SMS or email
  • To calculate pricing based on distance and route
  • To track flights for airport pickup timing adjustments
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Our drivers: Name, phone number, pickup location, and flight details necessary to complete your ride.
  • Service providers: Twilio (SMS), Google Maps (routing), AviationStack (flight tracking) — subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Retention

Booking records are retained for accounting and tax purposes as required by Florida and federal law (typically 7 years). You may request deletion of non-essential personal data by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption, secure session management, and access controls to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

We use session cookies to maintain your login state (admin/driver portals). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of SMS notifications by replying STOP

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
